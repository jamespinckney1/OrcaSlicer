## Things I was unsure about

- **Chamber temperatures**: QIDI's current pages almost never publish a number. Values for ABS Rapido (50), ASA (50), ABS Rapido Metal (45), ABS-GF25 (45) come from the legacy table on the Chinese wiki (the English wiki replaced it with a link hub). Odorless ABS (55-80, recorded 55), PC/ABS-FR (60-80, recorded 70) come from product pages; PPS-CF (60-65, recorded 65) from QIDI's blog. PET-CF's TDS says room-temperature chamber (enclosed, heater off). PA12-CF, PAHT-CF/GF, UltraPA(-CF25), PET-GF, PPS-GF20, S-White: enclosure required but no chamber number published, so null.
- **Densities not on current product pages**: PLA Matte Rapido (1.42), PLA-CF (1.25), PETG-Tough (1.24), ABS Rapido (1.05), Odorless ABS (1.05), ABS-GF25 (1.15), ASA (1.07), TPU95A-HF (1.15), TPU-Aero (1.15 raw; page lists 0.74 for the foamed part), S-White (1.23) are from the legacy wiki table. ASA-Aero density is not published anywhere (foamed) and is null.
- **Conflicting ranges between QIDI sources**: ABS Rapido nozzle 220-250 (current guide) vs 250-280 (legacy wiki); ASA 240-280 (product image) vs 240-260 (guide) vs 260-280 (legacy); PC/ABS-FR nozzle 260-280 / bed 70-80 (product page) vs 240-270 / bed 100-110 (guide and legacy); PET-CF 270-300 (current TDS "PET-CF17") vs 280-320 (older page text); PET-GF 260-320 (page) vs 280-320 (guide); S-White 260-280 (guide) vs 280-300 (page); PLA Basic / PLA Matte Basic 190-240 (page) vs 210-230 (guide). JSON records the product-page figure first and the alternative in notes.
- **Fan**: PLA-CF page says 80-100 % but the guide says 0-50 %; PETG Translucent page says turn all fans off; PAHT-CF and PET-CF TDS say fan off while the guide allows 0-50 %. Recorded the product-page/TDS value.
- **Speed**: the comparison guide's speeds (e.g. PLA Basic 30-50 mm/s, PETG Basic 30-50 mm/s) are much lower than the product pages (20-300 / 20-250). QIDI notes speeds are "for reference only; QIDI printers use the default profile". Recorded the product-page maximum where available.
- **Nozzle material**: PLA Matte Rapido was "hardened steel" in the legacy table but "All Material" in the current guide (recorded not required). PLA Wood lists hardened steel first on its page but "All Material" in the guide (recorded not required, abrasive=false). UltraPA page lists hardened first; guide says any (recorded not required).
- **Spool weights**: store weights are shipping weights (1300-1500 g); net 1 kg assumed from the "1 kg" variant labels; PPS-CF and PPS-GF20 are 750 g, PEBA 95A 800 g, UltraPA-CF25 labelled 1000 g. ASA-Aero and PC/ABS-FR variants carry no weight label (1 kg assumed).
- **Material enum mapping**: PETG-GF recorded as PETG (variant notes GF); PET-GF under PET-CF; PPS-GF20 under PPS-CF; ASA-CF20 Core under ASA; PC/ABS-FR under PC; PEBA 95A and S-White under OTHER. Abrasive/hardened flags are set from fibre content regardless of the mapped enum.
- **Status**: PLA Wood and PC/ABS-FR variants showed as unavailable in the store feed at retrieval but are still listed.
